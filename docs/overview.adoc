= Time for real applications

xref:index.adoc#manual[Manual home]

roc-time helps you answer questions such as “When is this room free?”, “When is this invoice due?” and “Which appointments belong to next week's schedule?” It keeps dates, elapsed time and bookings distinct, so you don't have to recover their meaning from bare timestamps.

If you know some Roc, you can xref:getting-started.adoc#getting-started[run your first application] with a released examples archive. You don't need to build the library first.

== Why intervals, not just instants?

A booking occupies time between two boundaries. To find availability, subtract that time from opening hours and keep every gap that remains. `Coverage` holds those pieces for you.

Each span includes its start and excludes its end. Two bookings can meet at 10:00 without overlapping: `[09:00, 10:00)` followed by `[10:00, 11:00)`. You don't need a “last microsecond of the morning.”

Sometimes you also need to know whose booking it is. Two customers can book the same window; their events keep separate identities even when their occupied coverage is equal. Likewise, an archive date of “June 2026” keeps month precision rather than becoming a recorded instant at midnight on June 1.

== What it looks like

An opening window is 09:00–17:00 UTC. A booking arrives as 12:00–14:00 at UTC+02:00, which occupies 10:00–12:00 UTC. Subtracting it leaves *09:00–10:00 and 12:00–17:00 UTC*. A single earliest-start/latest-end pair would accidentally fill the gap.

The https://github.com/lukewilliamboswell/roc-time/tree/main/examples/booking_exchange[booking application source] parses inputs, subtracts bookings and prints UTC timestamps. The xref:getting-started.adoc#getting-started[first application guide] shows how to run the release's copy.

== Explicit choices, useful guarantees

Exact calculations use signed 64-bit integer microseconds and checked arithmetic. Your application supplies a clock reading or timezone rules when it needs them. For repeated local times, you choose which occurrence an appointment means. For a long schedule, you choose how much work one query can do and resume it if necessary.

xref:profiles.adoc#profiles[Profiles and limits] describes the formats and operations supported today. Parsing, display, explanation and storage serve different purposes; a readable label doesn't replace the original declaration or its interpretation context.

== Prior art
